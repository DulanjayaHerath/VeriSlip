## Verified MERCon 2026 requirements

Checked against the official pages on 2026-10-04:

- [Author guidelines](https://mercon.uom.lk/call-for-papers/): English; official
  IEEE conference template; maximum six printed A4 pages including references;
  double-blind initial manuscript; no author, affiliation, grant, or personal
  acknowledgement information; PDF submission through EDAS.
- The same guidelines say review papers, literature summaries, tutorial-style
  manuscripts, and project reports are not accepted. The final paper therefore
  needs an executed experiment and novel research contribution.
- [Camera-ready instructions](https://mercon.uom.lk/camera-ready-paper-instructions/):
  IEEE template, six-page maximum including references, and IEEE PDF eXpress
  validation. Camera-ready-only identifiers and instructions must be rechecked
  for the actual target edition.
- [Important dates](https://mercon.uom.lk/important-dates/): the 2026 submission,
  camera-ready, and conference dates have passed.

No current ICTer rule is asserted here because an accessible authoritative page
was not verified during this change. Recheck the selected ICTer edition directly.

## Research evidence gates

- [ ] Record dataset authorization, consent, retention, and redaction process.
- [ ] Freeze a checksum manifest and dataset/schema version.
- [ ] Document genuine/manipulated counts without exposing PII.
- [ ] Use source/template/merchant/device-aware splits and check duplicates.
- [ ] Lock configuration, code commit, checkpoint hash (if any), and random seeds.
- [ ] Execute document, localization, calibration, latency, and subgroup metrics.
- [ ] Add confidence intervals and a statistical comparison plan.
- [ ] Run layer ablations and declared baselines under the same protocol.
- [ ] Audit false positives, false negatives, and benign transformations.
- [ ] Replace pending table cells from retained machine-readable outputs only.
- [ ] Confirm every claim is supported by a table, figure, citation, or code path.
